What is The Box?

I used to have an impulse buying problem and in order to help myself with that, I created The Box (with Tembo's help as you can see above!). I wanted to make impulse buying less rewarding for myself, so any plushie that I bought in a moment of weakness I wrapped and put inside The Box. Then, on a difficult day when I needed some comfort, the idea was to take a plushie out of The Box instead of scrolling Vinted and impulse buying new ones. It works well, because in moments like those, what I really need is self-compassion. Nowadays I've got much more control and I haven't impulse bought pretty much at all in 2026! Of course I still have moments of weakness when I see a cute plushie, but now I can take a deep breath and remind myself that though I certainly have enough love for all the plushies in the world, I don't have enough space, and someone else will love them just as much as me. This helps me curate my collection and save space and money for the plushies that I want the most!

What is The Waiting Room?

The Waiting Room is a similar concept. Every new plush I receive, be it through a trade, as a gift, because I saved up for it, because I made it, or through any other method, either goes into The Box or into The Waiting Room if they are too big for The Box! The difference between The Box and The Waiting Room is that I can grab something from The Box whenever I feel bad, but I can only take one plushie from The Waiting Room every month. I implemented this to keep my collection from growing super quickly, because though I didn't buy very many plushies in 2026, I still added a ton to my pack through gifts and trades. I wanted to feel a bit more in control over how much and how quickly my collection grows and I also wanted to feel excited again about adding a new plushie to my pack! I think it should be a super fun and special experience to get a new plushie, and because I have gotten as many as I have, I've lost that feeling, so here's to hoping this will help me capture that again.

Collection Updates

07/08/2026 - Taliesin joins the pack!

I just received a wonderful guardian wolf from a dear friend of mine. He was custom-made for me for a very special plushie that I got to make for my friend in exchange, none other than Njörun the soul fox! Taliesin was named after Merlin from the Arthurian legends, his other name in the book I'm currently reading! It suits this pretty pup very well. I am in awe of how beautiful he is! So much love, time, and effort went into his design and it shows in everything! From all the little details, like the thread sculpting and the striking eyes, to the way he was stuffed, so nicely weighted and wonderfully floppy, plus his incredibly soft fabrics, I marvel at him! I am so blessed to add him to the pack today. Welcome home, Taliesin!

30/07/2026 - Aleara joins the pack!

I am unbelievably happy to announce that Aleara is joining my pack! I thought I'd never find a WWF large silver fox for under a thousand, which I could never pay, so it means so much to me that a dear friend of mine decided to sell their second WWF silver fox to me for a much more reasonable price. UPS was unbelievably unclear about when she would be delivered, but finally she was dropped off at a nearby pick-up point so I was able to retrieve her today! I still cannot believe that she is actually mine, even as I have her on my lap as I'm writing this. I love her so much, welcome home Aleara!
